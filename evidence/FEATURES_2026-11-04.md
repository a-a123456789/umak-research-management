- PostgreSQL pg_trgm GIN title search, generated NFKC/lowercase/punctuation normalization, bounded pagination and year/department filters.
- Staff-only duplicate review including private drafts; public search retains Approved-only visibility and substring fallback.

## Benchmarks

[Measured report](../benchmarks/results/features-2026-11-04T14-45-09-443Z/report.md), [raw samples](../benchmarks/results/features-2026-11-04T14-45-09-443Z/samples.json), [per-seed summaries](../benchmarks/results/features-2026-11-04T14-45-09-443Z/summary.json), [query plans/build costs](../benchmarks/results/features-2026-11-04T14-45-09-443Z/query-plans.json), [environment/source hashes](../benchmarks/results/features-2026-11-04T14-45-09-443Z/metadata.json).

45 method-paired cases across five seeds, 30 timed repetitions per method/case and five warmups: **2,700 timing samples**. All output comparisons passed before timing and recorded source hashes match the measured files.

At 10,000 tickets the mixed heap workload (build, deadline updates, cancellations and drain) took median 5.992 ms versus 123.451 ms for repeated minimum scanning: median paired speedup **21.01x**. At 100 tickets the scan was faster (heap speedup 0.83x). This is not live SQL claim throughput and does not compare against one-time sorting.

At 100,000 titles, public search's six-query batch took 370.142 ms versus 914.492 ms exhaustive scoring: median paired speedup **2.47x**. Duplicate review took 437.067 ms versus 858.064 ms: **1.93x**. Each batch includes exact, typo, no-match, short and common queries; these numbers must not be presented as single-request latency. At 1,000 titles both proposed query forms were slightly slower than their exhaustive variants (0.92x). Representative selective plans use the GIN index at 10k/100k and scans at 1k, as chosen automatically by PostgreSQL.

Results concern one machine, synthetic corpora, warmup-based repeated query runs and local database access. They do not establish institutional duplicate accuracy, memory use, API p95/p99, concurrent throughput, calendar scheduling quality or production SLAs. The benchmark database used a temporary schema that was dropped after completion. Reproduction: [benchmark instructions](../benchmarks/README.md).
